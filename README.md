## V2.0 Changes
- You now need a [patch]([https://github.com/Reimittv/Survival-Revival-Patch-1.0](https://github.com/Reimittv/Survival-Revival-Patch-1.0/raw/refs/heads/main/FireRed_1.1_FasterFireRed_1.3.2_SR_QOL.ips)) that can be applied [here](https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/). 
- Bright Powder is no longer banned.
- Full-clearing the S.S. Anne now rewards a choice of 1 Super Potion or 1 Status Heal (this should be bought immediately upon disembarking).
- TMs no longer have the unique number rule, so now if you find TM39 on the ground and get it for beating Brock, you can use both copies. 
- Upon beating Erika, you now receive a Fresh Water (this can be used to heal or trade for a TM).
- Rotating Wild Encounters have been added to enhance the pivoting experience and encourage more runs.
- You now receive 16 Potions from the NPC north of Pallet Town, removing the need to buy them.
- Flute exchanges are now hardcoded, so selling a flute to a Poké Mart will provide the appropriate status heal in return.
- The Move Relearner has been moved from Sevii to Fuchsia (inside the Move Deleter's house) and may now be used as long as you have found the mushrooms to do so.  

## General Rules

ALL PREVIOUS [Standard, Ultimate, and Kaizo rules](https://gist.github.com/valiant-code/adb18d248fa0fae7da6b639e2ee8f9c1) apply (unless overridden by a Survival Revival rule below)!

**Super Kaizo-specific rules do not apply! No Safari Pivot.**

| Rules | Explanation | Notes |
| ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Swift Beginnings | ALL banned moves and held items may be used while labbing and pivoting. The normal restrictions apply once you begin the first non-rival battle. | This allows the use of moves like Spore to speed up catches. |   
| Swift Beginnings II | Pivoting is no longer restricted to one catch per grass; instead, you may catch as many total Pokémon as there are pivot locations (4 in FireRed). | You may still only catch one of that species per grass. (If I catch a Snorlax on Route 1, I cannot catch another Snorlax on Route 1 to look for a better nature or IVs.) |  
| Put That Down! | Any items acquired via the Pickup ability must be thrown away ASAP. | Having a held item equipped prevents picking items up anyway. |
| Limited Healthcare | After receiving your first Gym Badge, you MUST immediately use the Pokémon Center. You may now only use the Pokémon Center up to 5 times for the remainder of the run. | You may use beds, Doctors, the Healing Circle, and other PC substitutes; these still count toward your usages. |
| Final Frontier | After acquiring your 8th Gym Badge, you gain one bonus Pokémon Center usage. | |
| Power Limiter | You are allowed to use ONE (1) non-combat stat boosting move ONCE per fight. Calm Mind/Swords Dance/Iron Defense etc. when fighting a Gym Leader or the E4/Champion | Secondary boosts like Charge Beam and field effects like Light Screen, Mist, and Rain Dance are allowed. |
| Helpful Healing | As long as you are not inside a Gym or the Elite 4: HP heals, status heals, and PP items can be used outside of battle. While in a Gym or the Elite 4, healing must be done only in battle. | Dojos are treated the same as Gyms, so these restrictions apply there as well. |
| More Moves to Use | Moves that damage the enemy to heal yourself and moves that only cure status effects are now allowed to be used in trainer battles only, such as Leech Seed, Giga Drain, Aromatherapy, and so forth. Moves that heal HP without hurting the opponent, like Soft-Boiled, Morning Sun, and Wish, are still banned. | Pain Split is an exception and not legal. You may not use HP-healing moves, status-only healing moves, or Recycle in wild Pokémon battles. |
| Pogtion | You receive 16 Potions from the NPC north of Pallet Town instead of 1. | |
| BALLS of Knowledge | You may use all TMs acquired on your journey; they are 50% learnable. You may not purchase them from shops, but Gym Leader, NPC, ground, and held TMs are all allowed. | Held TMs can only come from your lab mon or a successful pivot; HM Friend items are totally unusable. |
| Trials of Knowledge | Full-clearing certain dungeons is rewarded with prizes. | Game-specific lists are available below. |
| PP Limit | You may only use 1 PP Up on each move. PP Maxes may not be used at all, but you may "trade in" each PP Max by selling it and buying a Hyper Potion. | When trading a PP Max, in place of a Hyper Potion, you may also opt for an equivalent amount of healing using other items like 4 Super Potions. Drinks are also allowed, such as Soda Pop and Lemonade. Max Potions and Full Restores are not. |
| The Tools of Survival | Held items may be used on your Pokémon from the beginning of the game and freely swapped between them. This includes Everstones to prevent evolving! | The following held items are still banned: Lucky Egg, Leftovers, Shell Bell, and Focus Band. You may still not cancel evolutions with the B button. |
| Bigger & Badder | All boss fights, Gym Leaders, and Pokémon League members have consumable held items. | Items stolen from these trainers via moves like Thief are usable as you please. |
| Diminishing Return | You may trade any 2 status-specific heal items for 1 status-specific heal item. | e.g., trading 2 Burn Heals for 1 Antidote. Full Heals cannot be traded for. |
| Flute Favors | Flutes may be sold to a Poké Mart in exchange for one corresponding status heal. | |

## Game-Specific Rules

### FireRed / LeafGreen

0. All other [FRLG-specific Kaizo rules](https://gist.github.com/UTDZac/a147c497424dfbd537d8c4b0c22b5621#fire-red--leaf-green) apply, unless changed below.
1. The Mt. Moon step items are now banned.
2. You no longer have to fight the rival on the S.S. Anne immediately.
3. Usable NPC TMs: Mr. Psychic in Saffron, the Return girl in the gate south of Lavender, and the dept. store girl by giving her a Fresh Water, Soda Pop, or Lemonade you have found. 
4. Available Dungeon Prizes: (One-shot dungeons still apply, so some Move Tutors will need to be used before leaving if desired.)  
  
| Dungeon | Prize |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Mt. Moon | You may learn from both of the Move Bros. |
| S.S. Anne | You may buy either a Super Potion or a status heal immediately upon disembarking. |
| Rock Tunnel | You may learn from the Youngster near the end of the tunnel. |
| Rocket Hideout | You may learn from the Celadon Counter Tutor (in the dept. store). |
| Silph Co. | You may learn from the Thunder Wave girl inside Silph Co. and the Copycat girl in Saffron (a Poké Doll is needed to use the Copycat one). |
| Cinnabar Mansion | You may learn from the Metronome Tutor in the research facility. |
| Victory Road | You may learn from the NPC near the exit, the Pewter City Museum guy, and the Viridian City Dream Eater guy. |
